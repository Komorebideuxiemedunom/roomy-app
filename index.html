Roomy

Free up space on your iPhone, honestly: duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and big videos. Back them up to your PC first, then delete. One purchase, no subscription.

Support

Deleted photos still use space? iOS keeps them 30 days in Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted. Empty it there to get the space back right away.

My PC does not appear for the backup. On Windows, install Quick Share (free, from Google) and open it. Keep the PC and the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi, and Roomy open.

Restore my purchase. Roomy › Settings › Restore purchase, with the same Apple ID.

Anything else: inceept@gmail.com
