Support

Deleted photos still use space?

iOS keeps them 30 days in Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted. Empty it there to get the space back right away.

My PC does not appear for the backup

On Windows, install Quick Share (free, from Google) and open it. Keep the PC and the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi, and Roomy open.

Restore my purchase

Roomy › Settings › Restore purchase, with the same Apple ID.
