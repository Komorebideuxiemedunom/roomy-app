Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

Roomy collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your photos

Roomy reads your photo library on your iPhone to find duplicates, similar photos, screenshots and large videos. The analysis (including image comparison with Apple's Vision framework) runs entirely on the device. Nothing is uploaded. Photos are deleted only when you ask, through Photos, which asks you to confirm.

Back up to your PC

When you choose it, Roomy sends the selected photos and videos directly to a device on your local network (for example a Windows PC with Quick Share), encrypted, without any server. Bluetooth is only used, if you allow it, to notice a device right next to you.

Purchases

Roomy Pro is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.
